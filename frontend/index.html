Physical Model & Theory Documentation

1. Governing Diffusion Dynamics

The solver models 1D ion transport within a thin-film electrode of thickness \(L\) subjected to cyclic potential sweeps \(V(t)\). The concentration distribution \(C(x,t)\) satisfies Fick's Second Law with potential-dependent diffusivity \(D(V)\):

$$\frac{\partial C(x,t)}{\partial t} = D(V(t)) \frac{\partial^2 C(x,t)}{\partial x^2}$$

Boundary conditions represent a blocking substrate at \(x = 0\) and equilibrium ion insertion at the electrolyte interface \(x = L\):

$$\left.\frac{\partial C}{\partial x}\right|_{x=0} = 0, \quad C(L,t) = \theta_{eq}(V(t))$$

2. Potential-Dependent Diffusivity \(D(V)\)

Diffusivity is asymmetric across the central thermodynamic threshold \(V_c\), modeled as:

$$D(V) = D_0 \exp\left(\beta(V) \cdot (V - V_c)^2\right), \quad \beta(V) = \begin{cases} \beta_L, & V < V_c \\ \beta_R, & V \ge V_c \end{cases}$$

3. Density of States (DOS) Representation

The equilibrium state function \(\theta_{eq}(V)\) is synthesized from a superposition of \(N\) Fermi-Dirac / error-function sub-bands:

$$\theta_{eq}(V) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} \frac{w_i}{1 + \exp(-k_i(V - V_{c,i}))}$$

The total Density of States \(DOS(V) = \frac{d\theta_{eq}}{dV}\) is the sum over individual Gaussian sub-band distributions:

$$DOS(V) = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i k_i \frac{\exp(-k_i(V - V_{c,i}))}{\left(1 + \exp(-k_i(V - V_{c,i}))\right)^2}$$

4. Multi-Stage L-BFGS-B Optimization Strategy

  • Stage 1 (Pure Flat Baseline): Fits the DC baseline current offset while masking high-curvature potential zones.
  • Stage 1.5 (Background Tails): Fits non-faradaic exponential charging tails (\(a_L, k_L, a_R, k_R\)).
  • Stage 2 (Anchor Peaks): Fixes diffusion coefficients and optimizes peak placements (\(w_i, V_{c,i}, k_i\)).
  • Stage 3 (Full Non-Linear Polish): Unfreezes all parameters simultaneously for global refinement.
